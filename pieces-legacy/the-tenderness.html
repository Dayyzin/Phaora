What Makes This Different

Small birds.
Gentle stone.

The Tenderness is the quietest composition in the PHAÖRA lineup — two small parrots on a pink opal base, pressed close together, both slightly smaller in scale than the mid-range pair pieces. The smallness is deliberate. Fabiano said it was about what is fragile, not what is grand.

Pink opal — milky, soft, with a warmth that differs from rose quartz in its opacity — gives the piece a quality of privacy. Unlike the more translucent pair pieces, The Tenderness does not invite the room. It exists for the person standing in front of it.
