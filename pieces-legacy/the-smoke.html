The Origin

Three macaws rendered in rich terracotta and brown quartz perch upon a raw white crystal cluster — a formation of milky quartz points that Fabiano selected for its density and the way its pale surface throws light onto the warm tones of the birds above. The contrast between the warmth of the macaws and the cold luminosity of the crystal base is not coincidence. It is craft.

Each bird is shaped individually — the angled wings, the curve of each tail feather, the weight distributed across gold-capped feet that grip the crystal as if the birds alighted moments ago. The trio has a hierarchy: one stands highest, one faces away, one watches the room. Together they animate any space they inhabit.
