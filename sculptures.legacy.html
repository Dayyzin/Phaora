Collection I

The Blue Macaws

Sodalite, lapis, and blue quartz macaws — set against amethyst, serpentine, and raw geode. The collection opens in deep ocean tones that shift toward violet as the light moves across the stone. Nine works.

Collection II

The Crimson Duo

Red jasper and smoky jasper set against cathedral-grade clear quartz. The most concentrated of the four collections — the rarest raw stone Fabiano works with. Four works.

Collection III

The White Cockatoos

Clear and white quartz set against deep amethyst and dark geode. Pure light against crystalline violet — the quietest of the four collections. Eleven works.

Collection IV

The Canopy

Rose quartz, aventurine, labradorite, and clear crystal — set on Brazilian driftwood, serpentine, and carved stone. The widest of the four collections, spanning statement flagships to entry works. Twenty works.
