Territory Boca Raton, FL

What we build outlasts trends because we begin where most finish — at the source. Our stone is quarried, not selected from a catalog. Our surfaces are set by hand, never assembled by machine. This is slow work, and we have no interest in accelerating it.

A house reveals its builder the way a shore reveals its tide — slowly, under pressure, and without apology.

We are not the largest firm in South Florida. We never intend to be. The work speaks when we do not.

— David Machado Vaz Founder, Phaöra
