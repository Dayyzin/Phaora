IIIthe aisle

Inside, the reel was running; three,the screen began, and counted down.The coat came quicker than need be,the only hurry in the town.

At two, the light came up to meet it.Ödara hung across a seat:black lamb in five, a face (don't greet it,it's made of holes) in cream, complete.

Ritornelo lay along the arm,two long black gloves, one seam, brass snap,left fingers-together, calm,the way you'd say, I'm coming back.
