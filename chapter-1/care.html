Chapter I

care

the knit

Wash cold, inside out, on a gentle cycle. Dry flat. No tumble dryer, no bleach.

UNCONFIRMED · the knitter confirms it

the silk

Dry clean. Steam on low from the inside; no direct iron on the satin. Keep out of strong light.

UNCONFIRMED · the silk maker confirms it

the leather

Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Keep from rain and heat; if it gets wet, let it dry slowly away from a radiator. Store in its dust bag, stuffed with tissue, out of the light. Leather cleaning by a specialist only.

UNCONFIRMED · the leather workshop confirms it

the gold

Keep it in its pouch, apart from other pieces. Wipe with a soft dry cloth.

For David: the care line for the gold hardware and the sunglasses, once the maker states the metal
