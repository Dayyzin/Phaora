A seat is kept for you

Vthe kept seat

The count went three, then two, then stopped.The screen would never say the one,for third along, one seat was kept:a card, a mark, a place for one.

An O, two dots, a line drawn through,no name; it didn't need one, quite.The coat sat down, the way coats do,all at once, then settled right.

The leather sighed; the sleeves found arms;eight buttons caught the light to stay,and held it close, like little charmsyou keep, and never give away.
