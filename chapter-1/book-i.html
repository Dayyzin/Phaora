Isnow

It snowed since four; the bulbs burned gold,the street had gone to hush and white,when down it came, ignoring cold,a coat that took its time tonight.

Gelosia: black lamb, cut thin,a net of diamonds, nine by seven,so cream silk glows from deep withinlike porch lights left on low in heaven.

Eight buttons, belt, a strap each cuff,a hem that swung to mid-calf, black;the snow tried hard, but not enough:it settled, sparkled, and slid back.
