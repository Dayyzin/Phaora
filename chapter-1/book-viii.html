VIIIhouse lights

The house lights rose, too soon, as they do.The row sat just the way it had:Coema's handles down; the gloves laid too;Coaraci's disc to the white screen, glad.

Not one had moved. That's what they do,the good films: leave you as you were.But where the coat had sat, a newsmall thing sat waiting in its stead:

no card. A box, black, square, and onits lid, a disc of gold, round as the onethe screen would never say. And gonethe coat, as if the night were done.
