The maker’s notes

Carbonado, the Quiet Black hoodie

  1. It begins as two black cottons: a matte one for the ground, a mercerised one for the dots.
  2. The dots sit on a 12 mm screen at 45 degrees and show only by their sheen.
  3. They meet as a checker at the hem band and thin out to plain black about 60% of the way to the neck.
  4. Toward the top, whole dots drop out one by one; none shrinks to a speck.
  5. The mark sits on the left chest, 6.0 by 4.8 cm, in the mercerised yarn.

Behind it: the hood and the rib, plain matte black.
