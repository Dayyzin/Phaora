The maker’s notes

Öfusca, the Star in Shades sunglasses

  1. The front begins as black acetate, about 5 mm thick: 136 mm wide, 49 mm tall.
  2. It takes two 52 mm lenses across a 16 mm bridge, grey, darker at the top.
  3. The temples are 130 mm, 11 mm tall at the hinge, on five-barrel steel hinges.
  4. A round gold plate, 8 mm, sits on the outside of each temple with the mark engraved 5.9 mm tall.
  5. They close into a black leather case, 164 by 60 by 52 mm, under a 22 mm gold disc.

Inside them: a flat steel core in each temple. Inside the case: cream suede.
