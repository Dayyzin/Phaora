The maker’s notes

Coema, the Matinee

  1. It begins as one panel of black calfskin, 2 mm thick, wrapped round a flat base and closed by two piped ends.
  2. It measures about 25 by 11 by 11.5 cm.
  3. One row of squares is pressed round the body, 0.45 mm deep, each square 43 by 45 mm.
  4. It is carried by two short rolled handles on brass rings, or by a strap 20 mm wide and 3.5 mm thick.
  5. It closes on a brass zip with two sliders, and stands on four brass feet.
  6. A leather charm, 23 by 38 mm, hangs from the front handle’s ring with a 12.5 mm brass plate.

Inside it: cream suede, printed with a couple in black halftone.
