Chapter I

care

the knit

Wash cold, inside out, on a gentle cycle. Dry flat. No tumble dryer, no bleach.

UNCONFIRMED · the knitter confirms it

the silk

Dry clean. Steam on low from the inside; no direct iron on the satin. Keep out of strong light.

UNCONFIRMED · the silk maker confirms it

the leather

Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Keep from rain and heat; if it gets wet, let it dry slowly away from a radiator. Store in its dust bag, stuffed with tissue, out of the light. Leather cleaning by a specialist only.

UNCONFIRMED · the leather workshop confirms it

the brass

Brass darkens slowly with wear; that is its nature. Wipe it with a soft dry cloth after wearing, and keep it from perfume, water and other metals. No polish on the leather around it.

the sunglasses

Keep them in their case. Clean the lenses with the cloth only, never paper. Fold the left temple first, then the right. Wipe the brass plates with a soft dry cloth.

öitava and lustre

The two pieces in 18k gold. Keep it in its pouch, apart from other pieces. Wipe with a soft dry cloth.

the house box

Every piece is delivered in the house box: black board, cream tissue, a brass disc on the band. Keep the box; a return travels in it.
