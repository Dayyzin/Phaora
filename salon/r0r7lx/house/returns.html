Chapter I

returns

reserving a piece

Made to order. We email your delivery date once production is confirmed. Cancel for a full refund any time before production starts.

returns

A piece may be returned within 14 days of delivery: unworn, with its tags, in the house box it came in.

To return a piece, write to hello@phaora.com with your name and the piece. The house answers with the address and the next step. The refund goes back to the way you paid, once the piece has arrived and been checked.

exchanges

The knitwear: size exchanges within 14 days of delivery, unworn and with tags.

Every other piece: exchanges within 14 days of delivery, unused and with tags.

what cannot be returned

A reservation cannot be cancelled once production has started. A piece that has been worn, marked or altered cannot be returned.

shipping

Each piece ships when it is finished, in the house box, tracked and signed for. The delivery date is confirmed by email before it leaves.
