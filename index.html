The water keeps moving.

The second collection took its name from the Portuguese for living waters — water that does not stand still, water that shapes what it touches. We worked with that principle throughout.

Living water does not reflect. It refracts. That is what we were after.

Eleven pieces. Each complete. When these are placed, the collection is closed.

— David Machado VazFounder, Phaöra
