§ Philosophy

A house begins
at the ground.

PHAÖRA is a studio of two crafts held in one hand: the hardscape that shapes the ground around a South Florida estate — pool decks, driveways, outdoor rooms — and the crystal works carved for its tables, mined and shaped by master Fabiano in the mountains of Minas Gerais, Brazil.

Both are the same practice at different scales — the patient cutting of stone until it holds light exactly. We do not finish a project until we have walked its stone at three different hours of the day.

```
I.

Sourced at the face

Every stone is chosen at the quarry, before it is cut. Every crystal is hand-mined in Minas Gerais. Nothing arrives by catalog.

II.

Hand-set, hand-carved

Pavers fit to a sheet of paper. Crystals shaped by forty years of devotion. There is no shortcut the work will forgive.

III.

One of one

No two projects alike. No two pieces alike. Once acquired, a work is gone from the catalog permanently.

§ Considerations

Before
you write.

01.
How long does a hardscape project take?

From first walk to final stone: typically four to twelve weeks for a residential project, depending on scope and material lead time. Larger estate builds run longer. We do not accelerate.

+
02.
Where do you accept commissions?

Primarily Palm Beach County — Boca Raton, Palm Beach, Delray Beach, Manalapan, Jupiter, Wellington, and the surrounding estates. For larger projects we travel throughout South Florida.

+
03.
Are crystal works available without a hardscape project?

Yes. The atelier releases its forty-four one-of-one works by request. A short note is enough to be added to the list for the next arrival from Minas Gerais.

+
04.
Do you work with designers, architects, and realtors?

Often. We have ongoing relationships with a small number of interior designers, custom builders, and luxury agents in Palm Beach County. Wholesale partner pricing is available for approved partners.

+
05.
What is the minimum scope you accept?

A single entry court, a single patio, a single sculpture. We have no minimum size — only a minimum measure of intent. Every conversation begins with David personally.

§ How we work

Four
measured
movements.

```
Movement I.

Walk

A day on your property, at three hours of light. No drawings, no brief. Listening to the ground and what it asks for.

Movement II.

Source

Stone chosen at the quarry. Crystal hand-mined in Minas Gerais. Every piece selected before it is cut.

Movement III.

Cut

Drawings become paper templates on the project floor, then full-scale mock-ups in the yard. Measured twice.

Movement IV.

Set

Our crew sets the work by hand. We do not leave until you can walk it in silence at three different hours.
