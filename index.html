Field notes

I
Below the frost line
New England ground moves in winter. A footing poured above the frost line lifts, and the wall on top of it cracks. Ours go below it — every time, no exception for budget.
II
Cure before load
Mortar hits handling strength in days but keeps gaining for weeks after. We don't load a wall or open a walkway early just to hit a schedule.
III
Tooled, not troweled flat
A concave joint sheds water off the face of the stone. A flush one holds it there. It costs seconds with a jointer, and it's why the wall stays dry.
