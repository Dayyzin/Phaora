Territory South Florida

Most firms in South Florida work from catalogs. We work from quarries. Every surface we deliver began as a conversation about the land beneath it — its grade, its drainage, its exposure to salt air. The material follows. The design follows. Nothing is rushed.

The stone was here before the house. We intend it to remain after.

We take fewer projects than we could. This is by design. Every commission receives the full attention of the studio, from first site visit to final seal.

— David Machado Vaz Founder, Phaöra
