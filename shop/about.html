About

One stone,
one answer

A block of crystal will only give up one shape. The work is finding it before the stone decides for you.

The stone

Every piece begins in Minas Gerais, the Brazilian state that has supplied the world's amethyst and quartz for two centuries. Stone is selected whole — for the way light moves inside it, and for the flaws, which are the part no one can reproduce.

The cut

Carved by hand, not cast. A mould would make the second one possible, and there is no second one. What the stone permits is what the piece becomes; where a vein runs wrong, the form changes to meet it.

Buying

Every work is one of one, so the bag is an enquiry list rather than a checkout. Send it and David answers personally — confirming the piece is still available, quoting crating and delivery, and arranging payment. Nothing is charged until you have spoken to him.
