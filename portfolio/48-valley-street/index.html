The Whole Job, One Frame
How It Got There6 steps
Step 01 · What was there

Gravel, weeds, and no edge

Loose stone with nothing holding it. Every rain moved some of it toward the street, and the bins sat on whatever was left.

Step 02 · Tear-out

All of it comes out

No paving over the old problem. The full depth came out so the new surface could sit on something that was actually built.

Step 03 · Excavate

Down to real material

Cut to grade and hauled off. This is where the pitch gets set — water has to leave the driveway on its own, forever, without being thought about again.

Step 04 · Base

The part nobody sees

Processed stone in lifts, compacted each time. This is the entire difference between a driveway that looks like this in fifteen years and one that doesn't.

Step 05 · Grade

Flat, and pitched on purpose

Screeded and checked before a single paver went down. Everything above this point is only as good as this photo.

Step 06 · Edge & lay

Set by hand, cut to fit

Pavers laid to pattern, then cut into every curve and corner and locked with a restrained edge so the field can never creep.
