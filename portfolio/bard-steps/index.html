How It Is Built

The treads are the easy part to see. What decides whether they are still level in ten years is underneath — every riser here is a course of laid stone, not a poured wall with a face stuck on it, and every tread is a single slab bearing across it rather than a slab bearing on its own ends. Nothing in the run relies on adhesive to stay where it was set.
