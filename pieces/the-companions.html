Why The Companions

Two birds. One conversation. Forever frozen.

The Companions captures something rare in sculpture — the feeling of relationship. Two parrots, perfectly balanced, suggest dialogue, warmth, and a bond frozen in crystal and time. Rose quartz brings luminous softness; amethyst brings depth and mystery.

This is the piece that collectors reach for when they want presence without severity — art that warms a room rather than commanding it.
