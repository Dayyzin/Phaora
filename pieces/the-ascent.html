Why The Ascent

One eagle. One stone. Total command.

Where other pieces celebrate multiplicity, The Ascent is defined by singular focus — one eagle, one moment, one stone. The rose quartz base radiates a warmth that transforms any room it enters, while the emerald serpentine ground adds depth and geological complexity.

The solo composition is the most demanding to execute. Every detail of the eagle is fully exposed, fully accountable. There is nothing to hide behind — only mastery to reveal.
