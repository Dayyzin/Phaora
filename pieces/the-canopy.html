Not art that fills a room.
Art that becomes one.

The Canopy is PHAÖRA's defining work — the piece that every collector who sees it asks about first. It is not a decoration. It is not an accent. It is the room's reason.

It is not a decoration. It is not an accent. It is the room's reason.

At 96 inches wide and 380 pounds, it is scaled for the spaces that deserve it: double-height lobbies, grand foyers, estate galleries, penthouse atriums. In every space it has occupied, the room was remembered because of it.

I
Seven individually carved birdsEach white quartz bird is a separate carving — not cast, not molded. Feather striations are cut by hand. No two birds are identical.
II
Atlantic rainforest driftwoodThe driftwood base was sourced from Brazil's Atlantic coast and treated to preserve its grain for generations. There is no substitute material.
III
Crystal botanical accentsFlowers and foliage carved from citrine, amethyst, and labradorite are worked into the driftwood at the junction points — visible only on close inspection.
IV
40 years in one pieceFabiano has been carving for over four decades. The Canopy represents the accumulated precision of that practice — it is not the work of a young artist.
